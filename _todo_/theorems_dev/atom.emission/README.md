#### the derivation

<sub>premise · emission/where-n-l-and-m-live</sub>  

AND l AND m ARE COUNTS ON THE SPHERE, used as a probability rather than as an amplitude. The source draws a direction and fires with chance |Y_lm|^2, then fires with chance |R_nl(t)|, and the two draws are independent to 1.001 - so what goes out is the product, with the harmonic's own smooth profile in it. |Y_lm|^2 vanishes l - |m| times in the polar angle and 2|m| times round the azimuth.
