#### the derivation

**share = \frac{opposed}{states}**  
<sub>definition · share.coherence</sub>  

two charges are opposed exactly when their meeting leaves nothing - that is what the word means for a rule that either annihilates a pair or does not. The states a pair can be in are equally available, so how much of the time they are opposed is how many of those states annihilate, over how many there are

**opposed = 1**  
<sub>premise · meeting/what-the-halves-do</sub>  

and left nothing at all in the other 1: those are the states in which the two were opposed, which is what being opposed MEANS in a theory whose meetings either annihilate a pair or do not

**states = 1**  
<sub>premise · meeting/what-the-halves-do</sub>  

a ray of G can be carrying nothing but itself, so a facing pair has 1 possible states and the enumeration is complete

**share = \frac{opposed}{states} = 1**  
<sub>derived · a ratio of counts</sub>  

```
share = \frac{opposed}{states}
= \frac{1}{1}
= 1
```

annihilating is 1 and cases is 1, both counted off the tiling, so the ratio is 1 exactly - and it is worth reading as annihilating/cases rather than as the number, because on another lattice it is a different number and the same ratio
