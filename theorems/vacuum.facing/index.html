the derivation

\(\delta \text{ goes every way alike}\) theorem · the lattice
exits(p): DEG ways out, none of them special

a point's exits are alike and a rule reads none of them by name, so what leaves a place goes every way alike

\(F = \frac{1}{2}\) theorem · isotropy leaves no mean heading
F = (1 - \hat{d} \cdot \hat{j}) / 2
\hat{j} = 0 where nothing is biased
F = 1/2

the facing factor is (1 - d^·j^)/2 and j^ is what the opposing population is doing on average. Alike in every direction, that is nothing - so a meeting in an undisturbed vacuum carries exactly a half, and the two limits it interpolates are one head-on and nought co-moving
