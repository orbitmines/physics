every source in the universe is putting charges everywhere. Adding up what all of them put on you, is there a total?
G on fcc-12
definition
Φ = \sumr dΦ

every source in the universe is putting charges everywhere, so what arrives at you is what all of them put together - taken shell by shell, out as far as there are shells

definition
dΦ = M(r) · δ/site

what one shell puts on you is how much matter is in it, times what a unit of matter at that distance puts on you - and the second of those is the falloff, cited rather than reproved

definition
M(r) ∝ ρ · shell

a shell at r steps has as many sites in it as the shell has, and matter is spread through space at some density - so the matter it holds is the one times the other. No power appears here; the shell's size is established separately

cited
δ/site ∝ A'rD-1·β

established earlier by gravity.falloff, on this same theory and lattice - the working is there rather than repeated here

multiplying
dΦ ∝ A'·shell·ρrD-1·β

each factor carries its own dependence and they multiply

premise
shell(r) = ball(r) - ball(r-1)

a site is either within r-1 steps or it is not, so the sites at exactly r steps are those within r less those within r-1 - a subtraction, with nothing left over and nothing counted twice

sites within r steps = 8
cited
ball(r) ∝ rD·β

established earlier by gravity.falloff, on this same theory and lattice - the working is there rather than repeated here

the difference of a dilate
shell(r) ∝ rD-1·β
shell(r) = ball(r) - ball(r-1)
= β·rD - β·(r-1)D
= β·rD - β·(rD - D·rD-1 + ...)
= β·D·rD-1 + ...
∝ β·rD-1

every site is either within r-1 steps or it is not, so the shell is the ball less its own shift by one step. Expanding (r-1)D by the binomial theorem, the leading β·rD cancels against the ball's, and what survives is D·β·rD-1. The D came DOWN from the exponent in that cancellation; a proportionality does not carry it, and the exponent left behind is D-1

expansion
dΦ ∝ A'·ρ

shell is not a primitive of this theory - it is what the line above shows it to be, so it stands in for itself here

summing over every shell
Φ → ∞
Φ = ∑∞r dΦ
dΦ ∝ A'·ρ
∑∞r r0 → ∞

what the shell puts on you goes as A'·ρm, so in r it falls off as r0 - which is to say it does not fall off at all. Every shell contributes the same, and there is no end of shells. So the total does not converge
