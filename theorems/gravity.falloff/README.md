**F = A · δ/site**  
<sub>definition · gravity.falloff</sub>  

what a body feels is what the medium has to offer where it stands, times how much of the medium it is open to. That is what a force IS in this model, and it mentions no distance

**F ∝ A·δ/site**  
<sub>derived · multiplying</sub>  

each factor carries its own dependence and they multiply

**δ is conserved in flight**  
<sub>premise · medium/what-transport-does</sub>  

run with MOVEMENT and ARRIVAL and nothing else, the number of rays in the world does not change: 1250, 1250, 1250, 1250, 1250, 1250, 1250. MOVEMENT writes each ray's contents onto exactly one neighbour and ARRIVAL makes that the ray, so transport is a bijection - it moves what it carries and neither makes nor unmakes any of it. This holds at every shell at once and for every run length, because it is a property of the rule rather than of a configuration. What was set aside to ask it: EMISSION, CREATION, ANNIHILATION, TRANSPORT - so this is what the medium does while CARRYING a disturbance, and anything those rules do to one is a correction on top of it

**δ travels through ρ**  
<sub>premise · medium/what-transport-does</sub>  

transport is the only rule that changes where anything is, and it moves along the lattice's own exits - one exit a tick, watched here as a front that never runs ahead of the steps it has taken. So a disturbance gets from here to there BY TRAVELLING THROUGH the medium, which is what makes the medium's own evenness a fact about the disturbance as well

**the lattice carries alike in every direction**  
<sub>premise · lattice/what-the-tiling-is</sub>  

the exit set's second moment is a multiple of the identity, so nothing carried on this lattice prefers a direction at second order

**δ goes every way alike**  
<sub>derived · carrying</sub>  

deficit travels through ρ, and ρ prefers no direction - so neither does what it carries

**δ ∝ S**  
<sub>definition · gravity.falloff</sub>  

the disturbance a body leaves is the rays it took out, and how many that is per tick is what S names. This is what the letter means, not something measured about it

**δ/site ∝ \frac{S}{shell}**  
<sub>derived · spreading</sub>  

conserved and even, so one site's share is the whole of it over the number of sites there are at that distance

**shell(r̄) = ball(r̄) - ball(r̄-1)**  
<sub>premise · lattice/what-the-tiling-is</sub>  

a site is either within r̄-1 steps or it is not, so the sites at exactly r̄ steps are those within r̄ less those within r̄-1 - a subtraction, with nothing left over and nothing counted twice

**ball(r̄) = |r̄·P ∩ L|**  
<sub>premise · lattice/what-the-tiling-is</sub>  

```
ball(r̄) = 1, 13, 55, 147, 309, 561, 923, 1415, 2057
Δ^(1) = 12, 42, 92, 162, 252, 362, 492, 642
Δ^(2) = 30, 50, 70, 90, 110, 130, 150
Δ^(3) = 20, 20, 20, 20, 20, 20
```

the sites within r̄ steps are the r̄-fold dilate of the sites within one step - take one step r̄ times and you are somewhere in r̄·P, and every site of r̄·P is reached that way. Checked: the counts walked out of the lattice are a polynomial in r̄ of degree 3, which is D, exactly and on integers

**ball(r̄) ∝ STEP·r̄^(D)**  
<sub>derived · Ehrhart's theorem</sub>  

```
ball(r̄) = |r̄·P ∩ L|
= β·r̄^(D) + c_1·r̄^(D-1) + ... + c_D
∝ β·r̄^(D)
```

Ehrhart 1962: the number of lattice points in the k-fold dilate of a lattice polytope is a polynomial in k of degree exactly D, whose leading coefficient is the polytope's volume. Here k is r̄ and P is the set of sites one step from the centre, so the ball's count is a polynomial in r̄ of degree D and everything below the leading term is dropped by the proportionality

**shell(r̄) ∝ STEP·r̄^(D-1)**  
<sub>derived · the difference of a dilate</sub>  

```
shell(r̄) = ball(r̄) - ball(r̄-1)
= β·r̄^(D) - β·(r̄-1)^(D)
= β·r̄^(D) - β·(r̄^(D) - D·r̄^(D-1) + ...)
= β·D·r̄^(D-1) + ...
∝ β·r̄^(D-1)
```

every site is either within r̄-1 steps or it is not, so the shell is the ball less its own shift by one step. Expanding (r̄-1)^(D) by the binomial theorem, the leading β·r̄^(D) cancels against the ball's, and what survives is D·β·r̄^(D-1). The D came DOWN from the exponent in that cancellation; a proportionality does not carry it, and the exponent left behind is D-1
