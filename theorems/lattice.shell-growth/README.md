# lattice.shell-growth

> how many sites of this lattice lie at exactly r̄ steps from a point, and what does that do as r̄ grows?

Proved under `G`, `G^XOR`, `G^XOR*2`. Generated by `implementations/.ts/src/theorems` - nothing here was written by hand: every premise is a count or an exact invariance taken from the theory itself, and the conclusion is what the inference rules made of them. [Open the page](index.html) to switch theory and lattice.

## G

### the result

**shell = β·r̄^(D-1)**

| lattice | D | DEG | box | ticks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fcc-12` | 3 | 12 | 21 | 20 |

#### the derivation

**shell = β·r̄^(D-1)**  
<sub>premise · lattice/what-the-tiling-is</sub>  

β is the leading coefficient Ehrhart gives the ball, so the shell it grows by is β·r̄^(D-1) - an equality at leading order rather than a proportionality with something dropped

#### what the runs found

`lattice/what-the-tiling-is` - holds. the ball is a polynomial of degree 3 in r̄ (D is 3), checked exactly by finite differences; ρ = 1.0000 per unit volume; the exit set is isotropic at second order

- volume of one fundamental cell = 1 - |det basis| for fcc-12, by elimination in 3 dimensions - from the basis vectors themselves, with no run and no fit
- sites within r̄ steps = 8 - walked over the lattice's own exits from the centre: 1, 13, 55, 147, 309, 561, 923, 1415, 2057 for r̄ = 0, 1, 2, ...
- degree of the count in r̄ = 3 - the 3-th difference of the counts is constant at 20, and a sequence whose 3-th difference is constant is a polynomial of degree 3. Integer arithmetic - ball(r̄) = 1, 13, 55, 147, 309, 561, 923, 1415, 2057  |  Δ^(1) = 12, 42, 92, 162, 252, 362, 492, 642  |  Δ^(2) = 30, 50, 70, 90, 110, 130, 150  |  Δ^(3) = 20, 20, 20, 20, 20, 20
- second moment anisotropy = 0.0000 - the spread of Σw·(c·p)^(2) over directions p, computed from all 12 exit vectors - 0 is perfectly even. Exhaustive over the exit set, so it holds for all time and every configuration rather than being sampled
