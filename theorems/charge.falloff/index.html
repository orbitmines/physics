the rays carry a sign, transport keeps it, and a round source puts out a net of it. How much of it is at one site, a distance away?

reached on fcc-12, cubic-6, square-4, line-2 - 4 lattices, G

No law follows for n[what a ray carries as polarity] from what the probes found here.

what was missing

The rules needed these and no probe established them here:

  • what a ray carries as polarity is conserved in flight
  • what a ray carries as polarity goes every way alike

That is a result about G, not a failure of the prover: a premise a run did not support is one this theory does not supply.
