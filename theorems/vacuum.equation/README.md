#### the derivation

**\Delta \narrow{l}{active} = \narrow{l}{neutral} + \narrow{l}{emits & vertex.source.spare} - \narrow{l}{active & next.active}**  
<sub>theorem · the rays at a local, as the rules narrow it</sub>  

```
ANNIHILATION: -\narrow{l}{active & next.active}
CREATION: \narrow{l}{neutral}
EMISSION: \narrow{l}{emits & vertex.source.spare}
\Delta \narrow{l}{active} = \narrow{l}{neutral} + \narrow{l}{emits & vertex.source.spare} - \narrow{l}{active & next.active}
```

l is a point and its rays at once, so every rule's pattern narrows l to what the rule fires on, and the line counts those narrowings in l's rays: what one firing lights or takes, over how many of l's rays one match is. Creation fires on a point - all its ways at once, which is why `neutral` is the point's and counts every ray of it. A meeting fires on an edge, which l sees from its own end as a lit ray whose next ray is lit too, and it takes that one ray. A source lights the ways it emits on, which already says it is not moving. And Δ is the movement: following a ray a step and a tick, through the space standing where it goes - the rule that settles each tick is what makes it a difference at all.

**\Delta \narrow{\forall}{@ == l} = \frac{\narrow{l}{active & next.active}}{2} - \narrow{l}{neutral}**  
<sub>theorem · the space at a local, as the rules narrow it</sub>  

```
ANNIHILATION: \frac{\narrow{l}{active & next.active}}{2}
CREATION: -\narrow{l}{neutral}
\Delta \narrow{\forall}{@ == l} = \frac{\narrow{l}{active & next.active}}{2} - \narrow{l}{neutral}
```

the same narrowings, counted in the points that stand at l. A meeting folds one of its two ends into the other, so l, seen from one end, gains half a point a meeting; and a creation hands one point back, whatever l holds - a firing is one event on one point. What the world grows behind a meeting or ahead of a ray is a new local, not a point standing here.
