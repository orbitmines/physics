**F_g = F_meet + F_vac**  
<sub>definition · gravity.full</sub>  

what a body feels is everything that arrives at it, and things that arrive add. So the gravitational force is the two channels together: the meetings between the bodies' own radiation, and the expansion the vacuum did not manage around them

<sub>definition · gravity.full</sub>
