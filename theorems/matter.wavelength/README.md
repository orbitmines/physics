# matter.wavelength

> a moving source has two retarded branches and you do not know which side you are on. What do they leave when they beat against each other?

Proved under `G`, `G^XOR`, `G^XOR*2`. ## G

### the result

**beat = 2·v**

| lattice | D | DEG | box | ticks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fcc-12` | 3 | 12 | 21 | 20 |

#### the derivation

**beat = ahead - behind**  
<sub>definition · matter.wavelength</sub>  

you do not know which side you are on, so both branches are there at half weight each and what you see is the two beating against one another - their difference. The halves cancel out of the difference, which is why the answer does not depend on them being exactly a half but the WAVELENGTH does

**ahead = 1 + v**  
<sub>definition · matter.wavelength</sub>  

the branch that set out ahead of the source is compressed by its motion - the retarded time carries a 1/(1 - v), which to first order in a small speed is 1 + v

**beat = 1 - behind + v**  
<sub>derived · substituting</sub>  

```
beat = ahead - behind
ahead = 1 + v
beat = 1 - behind + v
```

ahead is not a primitive of this theory - it is 1 + v, so it stands in for itself here and the result is multiplied out

**behind = 1 - v**  
<sub>definition · matter.wavelength</sub>  

the branch behind is stretched by the same motion by the same amount the other way - 1/(1 + v), which to first order is 1 - v

**beat = 2·v**  
<sub>derived · substituting</sub>  

```
beat = 1 - behind + v
behind = 1 - v
beat = 2·v
```

behind is not a primitive of this theory - it is 1 - v, so it stands in for itself here and the result is multiplied out

#### what the runs found

`counts/what-the-tiling-fixes` - holds. fcc-12: D = 3, DEG = 12, SHEET = 6, CYCLE = 6 - counts, not measurements

- D = 3 - how many dimensions fcc-12 has
- DEG = 12 - the ways out of a point on fcc-12 - the exits it has, counted
- SHEET = 6 - the exits with no component along the pulsing axis, which is what a source emits over. NOT DEG, which is 12
- CYCLE = 6 - how many steps go round the ring of a turn on fcc-12
- slice = 2 - a turn sweeps a PLANE, and a plane needs two dimensions - so what a turn lives in is min(D, 2), which is 2 here and stops growing at 2 however many dimensions are added
- c̄ = 1 - a step's worth of lean is what one annihilation buys a path, and a step is c̄ - one cell a tick, the lattice's own speed. Its value in those units is one, which is a statement about the units and not about the quantity
